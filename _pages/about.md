I am **Junteng Liu**, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and a member of the HKUST NLP Group. I am advised by Professor Junxian He, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
